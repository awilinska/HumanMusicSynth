## Human Music Synthetizer

01000111010001010100111001001111010101000101100101010000 (translated from binary: GENOTYPE) is an interdisciplinary project at the intersection of art and technology.

By combining electronics, medical sensors, a game engine, a sound synthesizer, and a visual layer generator, an installation was created that generates music and animation based on the medical readings of the person connected to it. 

Every person is different; no two readings are the same, and no two melodies are ever identical.

The prototype was created using an Arduino microcontroller with sensors connected to it. The readings are transmitted to the Unity game engine, where they are translated into an audiovisual layer.
